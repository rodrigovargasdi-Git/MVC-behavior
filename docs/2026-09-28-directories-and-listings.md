**Method and limits**
- Two example platforms (therapie.de, Autismus-Stiftung) and 6 platform or requirement pages were fetched on 28.09.2026. The rest come from web-search summaries.
- **Eligibility and prices change. Re-check each one on its sign-up page before paying.** Items marked *(verify)* were not confirmed on the platform itself.
- Nobody was contacted.

**Gate before any public listing:** a written side-activity approval from Der Steg and confirmed credentials (research doc §8, §10 Q1–Q2). **Listings are public and indexed**, so they are the most visible form of "competing activity". Until then, only update profiles that already exist (FTF directory, LinkedIn), and do so **without advertising services**.

## 1. The two examples Rodrigo found

### 1.1 therapie.de (Pro Psychotherapie e.V.): **Marija does not qualify**
- **Who is listed:** approbierte Psychologische Psychotherapeut:innen, Kinder- und Jugendlichenpsychotherapeut:innen, ärztliche Psychotherapeut:innen and **Heilpraktiker für Psychotherapie**. The Berlin autism filter shows about 148 detailed entries. Profiles show methods, languages, insurance (GKV/PKV/self-pay) and free places.
- **Requirement:** registration needs an **Approbation or a permission under the Heilpraktikergesetz**. The sign-up page mentions no category for pedagogues, coaches or behaviour analysts.
- **Cost:** free basic entry (3 bullet points), "kleine Seite" €69/year, "große Seite" €99/year.
- **Verdict:** a BCBA or FTF credential is not a German Heilkunde permission, so **she is not eligible**. The only route would be the *Heilpraktiker für Psychotherapie* exam. **Not recommended.** It would reframe her consultancy as Heilkunde (psychotherapy), trigger HWG rules on health advertising and clash with the "Beratung, not Therapie" positioning in research doc §8. **Priority: none.**

### 1.2 Autismus-Stiftung – Einrichtungsübersicht Berlin: **worth requesting**
- **What it is:** a nationwide, independent directory of autism services run by the Autismus-Stiftung, with about 120 Berlin entries. It covers diagnostics, therapy, early intervention, counselling, self-help, housing and work, and includes **private practitioners** as well as Träger (e.g. Charité, Lebenshilfe, Autismus Deutschland LV Berlin, LebensWelt). Each entry shows name, category and district.
- **Requirements:** no public application form or criteria. The foundation curates the list and invites corrections via its contact form (they answer on working days). **Route:** after launch, send a short, factual request through the contact form with the category ("Beratung / Elterncoaching"), languages and a website link. **Cost:** none seen. **Priority: high** (after the gate). It ranks for German parent searches and gives neutral third-party visibility.

## 4. Prioritised 30-day listing plan

**Day 0 (prerequisites, no public listing before these are done)**
- Written side-activity approval from Der Steg **(lawyer, Arbeitsrecht)**.
- Credentials confirmed: FTF level and expiry date, BACB status and number, degree names (Hanley doc §8).
- Standard assets ready:
  - square headshot (research doc §9.2 #3);
  - 50-, 150- and 400-word bios in DE/EN (HR/ES short versions);
  - the credential line from the Hanley doc §6;
  - the one-sentence offer: "Private behaviour consultancy for families and professionals – DE/EN/HR/ES – online and in Berlin".
- A UTM-tagged link per platform, so the website can count which listing brings inquiries.

| Week | Actions | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | (1) FTF directory: submit the change form (Berlin, Germany; website). (2) LinkedIn: headline, About, Services, "Licenses & certifications" (FTF level, BACB if active). (3) Google Business Profile: create it as a service-area business and start video verification. (4) Hilfelotse Berlin: free entry. | 4 live or pending profiles |
| **2** | (5) It's Complicated: choose Basic, pick a non-"therapy" category, set up the booking page with the free 15-min fit call. (6) Speakerinnen.org profile (DE/EN) with 3 talk topics: "PFA & SBT – compassionate ABA", "Autism support in Germany explained for expat families", "Mehrsprachige Familien und Verhalten". (7) ABA-D membership application. | 3 profiles, 1 membership |
| **3** | (8) Autismus-Stiftung: send the inclusion request via the contact form. (9) HKM Berlin: offer a free parent information evening (HR). (10) Berlinski magazin: pitch a portrait. (11) Ask iamexpat.de for its listing criteria and price. | 2 requests, 2 community contacts |
| **4** | (12) Check Psychology Today eligibility; list only if no "therapist"/Heilkunde label is required. (13) Contact My International Therapy about a referral partnership, plus 2 international schools' learning-support coordinators. (14) Spanish lists (espanoles-en, Berlín en Español) if her Spanish level is confirmed. (15) Note ABA-D, EABA and ABAI submission deadlines in the calendar. | Pipeline for B2B2C and speaking |

**Day 30 review:** listings live, inquiries per UTM source, fit calls booked. Keep what produced a fit call. Drop paid listings that produced none after 90 days (in line with the research doc §3.4 pass/fail logic).

**Ongoing hygiene**
- The FTF credential renews **every year**. Put the expiry date in the calendar, because an expired credential must disappear from every profile.
- BACB recertification cycle (if applicable).
- Keep all profiles word-for-word consistent with the website's credential line.
