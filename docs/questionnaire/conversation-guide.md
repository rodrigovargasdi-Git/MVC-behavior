# Conversation guide: Marija's website, round 2

**Why this replaces the rest of the PDF:** Marija's feedback (2026-09-28) was that it was very complicated, many things weren't really answerable, and some questions missed the mark. The PDF mixed business-setup decisions with questions about her work, and it asked her to think in marketing categories. This guide does two things instead:

- **Marija only talks about her work, in her own terms.** Talk for 20–30 minutes, over dinner or a walk, as voice memos in any language. Rodrigo records it on the phone and drops the audio or the transcript into `answers/` (git-ignored).
- **Rodrigo answers the business questions** (bottom of this page), together with a Steuerberater where needed.

No need to answer everything. Stories are better than definitions.

---

## For Marija (open questions, 20–30 min)

1. **A case from start to end** (no names, no details that identify anyone): when a family first comes to you, what is going on? What do you do in the first weeks? What has changed after a few months?
2. **What families say at the start.** In their words, what are they struggling with? What have they already tried?
3. **Your way of working.** If a parent asks "how is your work different from what we had before?", what do you answer?
4. **PFA and SBT in plain words.** How would you explain them to a tired parent in two minutes?
5. **Your day as a supervisor.** What do you build for therapists: programmes, milestones, data? What are you proud of?
6. **Your path.** Croatia, Earlham (USA), Linköping (Sweden), Germany. What did each step give you that you use today?
7. **Who you want to help first** with your own practice, and what you would *not* take on.
8. **The feeling.** After reading your website, what should a parent feel? What should a teacher or a Jugendamt case worker feel?
9. **Words.** Which words do you like for your work, and which do you dislike (e.g. "Therapie", "Training", "Förderung", "Begleitung", "ABA", "Verhaltensanalyse")?
10. *(Optional)* **You as a person.** Anything you'd like parents to know about you beyond the work.
