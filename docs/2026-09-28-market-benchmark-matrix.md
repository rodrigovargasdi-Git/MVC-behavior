## 1. Summary (read this first)

- **The market has two halves that do not meet.** On one side are large Berlin Träger funded by the Jugendamt (Der Steg, LebensWelt, Zephir, Autismus Deutschland LV Berlin). They are eclectic and TEACCH-led, use ABA only "as elements", work in German only and do not publish prices. On the other side are a few solo BCBAs working in English, spread over Germany, with thin credentials and no prices. **Nobody offers a premium, senior-led, multilingual consultancy with published packages in Berlin.**
- **Across 21 providers, no website mentions PFA, the IISCA or Skill-Based Treatment.** This was checked at page level. Hanley's approach is a **real method gap** in the German market.
- **Demand clearly exceeds supply.**
  - According to the DGSPJ, most autism therapy centres have waiting times of **1 year or more**.
  - The main Berlin adult diagnostic clinic accepts **fewer than 40 of 1,300+ requests per quarter**.
  - § 35a Jugendamt cases more than doubled in ten years (**174,000 in 2024, +115 %**), and spending tripled to **€3.5 bn**.
  - Diagnosed prevalence among 0–24-year-olds rose to **1.0 %** in 2024.
- **Languages:** 413,000 Croatian and 204,000 Spanish nationals live in Germany (Destatis, 31.12.2025), and 833,000 foreign nationals live in Berlin. But Croatian and Spanish autocomplete shows **almost no Germany-specific searches**. English queries such as "aba therapy berlin germany" and "autism therapist berlin" do appear. **English wins on search. Croatian and Spanish need community channels, not SEO.**

### 3.4 Five implications for Marija's offer and pricing

1. **Sell time-to-start, not hours.**
   - Put "Start within 14 days" and the **Clarity Consultation (from €240)** above the fold.
   - Add a **"While you wait" parent programme** (6–8 weeks, parent-mediated, no diagnosis needed because it is Beratung, not Diagnostik or Therapie). Families stuck on 1-year waitlists get a concrete next step.
   - Never promise to replace funded Förderung or to speed up a diagnosis. **Do not market to Der Steg's waitlist** (research doc §8).
2. **Make PFA-SBT the signature, packaged by outcome.**
   - For severe or persistent challenging behaviour, the "Calm Days" programme becomes "PFA-SBT Programme" (8–12 weeks, from €1,350; the Home Intensive from €4,900).
   - Nobody else in Germany names the method, so the method name can carry a premium. Use it **only in line with her verified FTF level** (see `2026-09-28-hanley-pfa-sbt-positioning.md`).
   - Publish "ab" prices. Only 2 of 21 providers publish full prices, so transparency is itself a trust signal.
3. **English is the main public language, HR/ES the community languages.**
   - Full DE + EN pages, SEO-targeted at the Berlin English queries.
   - HR and ES as short pages with the **same prices**, offered **online Germany-wide**.
   - Reach HR and ES families through community channels (Croatian Catholic missions, Croatian supplementary schools, Spanish-language directories) rather than paid SEO, because autocomplete shows no diaspora search demand.
4. **Build a B2B line for the §35a boom, but time it after the employment question.**
   - Träger face record caseloads, staff shortages and no current AWMF therapy guideline.
   - Offer in-house **PFA-SBT workshops, team coaching and case consultation** (day rate derived from the research doc §7 senior rate of about €150–170/h, **proposal**).
   - Training **Berlin Träger that compete with Der Steg** while she is employed there is the highest-risk activity. Start with international schools, Kitas, HR/ES organisations and online groups **(lawyer)**.
5. **Treat verifiable credentials as part of the product.**
   - The market shows unclear credentials (Early Learning, Developing Behavior).
   - Show each credential with its awarding body, level, year and a verification link: the FTF directory, the BACB registry with the BACB disclosure for Germany, and the ZAB statement of comparability for a US degree if she gets one.
   - Price **professional supervision** only if her FTF level permits it (Level 6+). Level 4 supports "implementation", not "supervision" or "training of implementers".
