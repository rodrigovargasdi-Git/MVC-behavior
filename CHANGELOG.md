# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-09-25 — static Astro rebuild
- Rebuilt as a static German Astro site in `site/`, following the agent-team evaluation.
- 13 pages: Start, Leistungen (+ Familien, Fachkräfte, Institutionen & Jugendämter), Fortbildung & Supervision, Über Marija, FAQ, Kontakt (+ Danke), Impressum, Datenschutz, 404.
- Blockers fixed: the contact form really sends (configurable endpoint, honest fallback, no fake success); Impressum and Datenschutz templates exist; fonts are self-hosted, with no Google requests and no cookies.
- SEO/AEO: static HTML, `lang="de"`, per-page meta, canonical, Open Graph, JSON-LD, sitemap, robots.txt, `llms.txt`.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast on all text (min. 4.96:1), skip link, focus states, reduced motion, 375 px layout.
- ABA positioning is open and honest: named, with a "Wie ich arbeite" list and an FAQ answer.
- All unconfirmed facts are centralised as `[[BITTE BESTÄTIGEN]]` placeholders in `site/src/content/site.ts`.
- Removed the Replit monorepo (Express API, Postgres, mockup sandbox, Linux-only build). It is archived as tag `v0.1-replit-draft`.
